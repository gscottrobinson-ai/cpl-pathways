Stage 3

Checks before any credit posts

These come from NSU policy and apply to every path.

50% capCPL can't exceed half the minimum credits for the degree.
ResidencyCPL doesn't count toward the 30 upper-division credits students must earn at NSU.
No duplicatesNo credit for learning already credited by a course or another CPL award.
GPA and enrollmentCPL doesn't affect GPA, semester enrollment, or financial aid calculations.
SAP checkApply credit where it advances the degree. Blanket general electives can create SAP risk in tight programs like Nursing.
AppealsStudents appeal an evaluation to the dean over that subject area.
